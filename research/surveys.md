# Survey reference

Three Google Forms created for the validation phase. Content captured below
(English branch) for reference — the forms themselves are the source of truth
for anyone filling them in.

Review notes / potential issues are at the bottom of this file.

## 1. Goalkeeper Market Research — "🥅 Goalkeepers Wanted - Quick Survey"

Link: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdbSFpazWcHyMFnRPUdZXs0qQwdzJQ7on6WkVLPSCrbP5V-Pw/viewform

Intro: *"We are studying the interest of football goalkeepers in occasional
paid matches. This survey takes less than 1 minute."*

Languages offered: English / Nederlands / Francais.

Questions (English):

1. Have you ever had a football match where you couldn't find a goalkeeper? — Yes / No
2. Where do you usually play football? *(checkbox)* — Ghent / Antwerp / Brussels / Bruges / Leuven / Other
3. Do you regularly play as a goalkeeper? — Yes / Sometimes / Never
4. Would you be interested in earning extra money by playing occasional matches? — Yes / No / Maybe
5. What would be a fair payment for one match? — €15 / €20 / €25 / €30
6. How much notice would you need? *(checkbox)* — Same day / 24 hours / 2-3 days / One week or more
7. How far would you be willing to travel? — Up to 5 km / Up to 10 km / Up to 20 km / More than 20 km
8. What would motivate you most? *(checkbox)* — Extra income / Playing more football / Meeting new people / Staying active
9. Would you like to be contacted if the project moves forward? — Yes / No
10. E-mail or Whatsapp (Optional) — free text

## 2. Teams Market Research — "⚽ Amateur Football in Belgium – Quick Survey"

Link: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdg892YpGgwLlgc5ESKWGUAWUFXUdwZkAAV2lSCb_fj04GWPw/viewform

Intro: *"We're conducting a short study about amateur football and the
challenges teams face when organizing games. This survey takes less than 1
minute."*

Languages offered: English / Nederlands / Francais.

Questions (English):

1. Have you ever had a football match where you couldn't find a goalkeeper? — Yes / No
2. Where do you usually play football? *(checkbox)* — Ghent / Antwerp / Brussels / Bruges / Leuven / Other
3. What type of football do you usually play? *(checkbox)* — With Friends / Amateur League / Corporate football / Tournaments / Other
4. How often does your team struggle to find a goalkeeper? *(checkbox)* — Never / Every month / Almost every week
5. What do you usually do when your goalkeeper is unavailable? *(checkbox)* — Someone else goes in goal / We ask friends / We ask in WhatsApp/Facebook groups / We cancel the game
6. If a reliable goalkeeper could be found for your match, would you consider paying for a service that provides these goalkeepers? — Yes / No / Depends on price
7. How much would your team be willing to pay for a goalkeeper? — €15–25 / €25–35 / Other (free text)
8. Would you like to be informed if such a service becomes available? — Yes / No / Maybe
9. E-mail or Whatsapp (Optional) — free text
