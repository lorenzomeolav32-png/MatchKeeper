# GK_service — Goalkeeper-on-demand for amateur football (Belgium)

> Status: **validation phase**. No product exists yet. This repo tracks market
> research, surveys, and (later) the landing page / waiting list.

## The problem

Amateur football matches (friends, amateur teams, corporate teams, tournaments)
regularly get disrupted when the goalkeeper cancels last-minute or none was
arranged. Teams currently cope by asking friends, posting in WhatsApp/Facebook
groups, playing without a keeper, or cancelling the match. No specialized
platform for finding on-demand goalkeepers appears to exist in Belgium.

## The idea

Connect teams/groups needing a goalkeeper with available goalkeepers nearby:
team requests → sent to nearby available goalkeepers → one accepts → match
goes ahead. Fully manual for now (no app) — this phase is pure market
validation.

## Market

- Country: Belgium. Launch city: **Ghent**. Future: Antwerp, Brussels, Bruges, Leuven.
- Demand side: groups of friends, amateur teams, corporate teams, tournament
  organizers, football communities.
- Supply side: amateur/semi-competitive goalkeepers, students, expats, casual players.

## Long-term vision

Could expand beyond goalkeepers to replacement players and referees. First
version stays scoped to goalkeepers only — the problem is clear and easy to
communicate.

## Core value proposition

Never cancel a match because your goalkeeper is unavailable. Play more
competitive friendly games with good goalkeepers.

## Positioning rules

- Honest, community-driven, simple, football-focused, friendly, local, practical.
- Do **not** present the service as fully operational or claim an existing
  large goalkeeper network yet.
- No startup buzzwords, no AI talk, no "Uber of football" framing.

## Current stage / next steps

1. Collect data via 3 Google Forms (see [research/surveys.md](research/surveys.md)).
2. Build an Instagram presence.
3. Send forms out (WhatsApp/Facebook groups, personal network in Ghent).
4. Review results → decide go/no-go before building anything else (landing
   page comes later, deliberately not built yet).

## Repo structure

- `research/` — survey content, responses/findings, notes from manual outreach.
